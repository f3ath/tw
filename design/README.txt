Open TorqueWrench-Brandbook.pdf for the visual and usage guide.
Page 4 lists light and dark service badge colors.

svg/
  logo-*          Horizontal symbol and outlined wordmark. Use at 160 px or wider.
  symbol-*        Standalone marks with transparent outer corners. Use the small versions below 48 px.
  *-light         Petrol tile with an ink wordmark on light backgrounds.
  *-dark          Petrol tile with a mist wordmark on dark backgrounds.
  *-mono-dark     Ink on light backgrounds.
  *-mono-light    White on dark backgrounds.
  app-icon*       Opaque petrol square. The operating system can supply the mask.

png/
  1024, 512, 192, 48 px  Full app icon.
  32, 24, 16 px        Compact app icon with bolder, wider-spaced arcs.

All SVG artwork is self-contained. The lockups use outlined type.
